# Math Review Notebook

[简体中文](README.md) · **English** · [Español](README.es.md) · [日本語](README.ja.md)

[Open the online reader](https://hokkaidoooo.github.io/math-notebook/)

A Chinese-language notebook for reviewing math problems. Browse 35 topics or search by keyword across 2,611 problems and notes. Review solutions, common mistakes, related concepts and formulas, and zoom into the original problem images.

## Read online

Open the reader on your phone or computer. Images load as needed over Wi-Fi or mobile data. No account, command-line setup or image import is needed for online reading.

## Read offline on Android

1. In the reader, open **在线阅读** (Online reading) in the top right and find **离线阅读** (Offline reading). You can also get `math-notebook-android-offline.zip` from the [Android offline release](https://github.com/Hokkaidoooo/math-notebook/releases/tag/offline-20261117). The download is about 480 MB.
2. Extract the outer ZIP on your phone and open `数学错题本_手机版.html` in Chrome.
3. Select `题图资料包.zip` inside the extracted folder. Leave this inner ZIP intact. Select it again each time you reopen the offline reader.

## Privacy and credits

Favorites and mastery marks are stored in your current browser. The online and local offline readers keep separate records; marks do not sync between them or across devices.
